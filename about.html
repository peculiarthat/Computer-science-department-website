WHO WE ARE

Developing the Next Generation of Technology Professionals.

The Department of Computer Science is part of Lens Polytechnic, Offa, Kwara State, an institution committed to building manpower and developing entrepreneurs through practical and industry-ready education.

Our department focuses on equipping students with foundational and practical knowledge in computing. Through learning in programming, software development, computer systems and other areas of information technology, students are introduced to the skills needed to understand and solve real-world technological challenges.

We believe that effective computing education goes beyond theoretical knowledge. It involves creativity, practical experience, critical thinking and the ability to apply technology to everyday problems.
